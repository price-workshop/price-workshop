## PRICE Workshop History

The PRICE Workshop series has been held annually at SIGGRAPH since 2021, exploring the technical challenges of competitive esports from multiple angles.

## Previous Editions

### [Esports Rendering: Industry and Player Perspectives — 2025](/2025/)
SIGGRAPH Frontiers Workshop. Focused on industry and player perspectives on rendering in esports.

### [Less is More: Rendering for Esports — 2023](https://research.nvidia.com/publication/2023-08_less-more-rendering-esports)
SIGGRAPH Frontiers Workshop. Explored minimal rendering techniques for competitive gaming. Also featured a [SIGGRAPH webinar](https://blog.siggraph.org/2023/07/webinar-the-evolution-of-esports.html/).

### [The Esports Frontier: Rendering, Interaction and Display — 2022](https://research.nvidia.com/publication/2022-08_esports-frontier-rendering-interaction-and-display)
SIGGRAPH Frontiers Workshop. Covered the frontiers of rendering, interaction, and display technology in esports.

### [Esports as a Driving Problem in Computer Graphics — 2021](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3450617.3464499)
SIGGRAPH Panel. Established esports as a compelling application domain for computer graphics research.
